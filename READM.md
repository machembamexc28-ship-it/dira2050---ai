# Dira2050 AI Assistant 🇹🇿

Dira2050 AI Assistant is an AI-powered platform designed to help
Tanzanians understand, explore and participate in Tanzania's
Development Vision 2050.

## Vision

To make Tanzania Development Vision 2050 accessible, understandable
and actionable for every Tanzanian.

## Core Features

- 🤖 AI Assistant
- 📚 Dira 2050 Knowledge Base
- 🎯 Personal Goals & Opportunities
- 💡 Innovation Ideas
- 🎓 Student & Youth Support
- 🏢 Business & Investment Opportunities
- 📊 Vision 2050 Progress Insights
- 🇹🇿 Swahili-first experience

## Project Status

Dira2050 AI Assistant — MVP Development

## Mission

Turning Vision 2050 from a document into an interactive digital
experience for Tanzanians.
